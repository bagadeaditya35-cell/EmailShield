## What every stored finding contains
rule id, title, points, severity, group, reason, evidence (the matched values), recommended next step, rule version; every scan also stores engine version, ruleset fingerprint, score, category and timestamp. MITRE ATT&CK mappings are derived from fired rules (see attack.py).

## What the engine does NOT do
Content/semantic analysis, image/QR analysis, sandboxing, URL fetching, reputation lookups (unless the optional threat-intel providers are enabled), or any action on the mailbox.
